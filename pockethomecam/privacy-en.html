PocketHomeCam Privacy Policy

First effective: 10 September 2026 · Revision announced: 10 November 2026 · In effect: Cloudflare correction 10 November 2026, website items 10 December 2026 · Terms of use · Account and data deletion · 한국어

Minarae ("we") operates PocketHomeCam (the "Service"), an app that turns a spare phone into a home camera you watch from another phone or a web browser. This policy explains what we process, why, for how long, and your rights.

Three principles
  • Wi-Fi connections do not pass through our server. When two phones connect directly on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot, no video, location or account data reaches our server, and no sign-in is needed.
  • Video only, no sound. The Service does not use the microphone or process audio. Over the Pro relay, live video is passed through and is stored on our server only when the viewer records to the Online Box. If the detection photo feature is on, detection photos are also sent to our server (section 1). Recordings made on the camera phone stay on that phone.
  • No covert filming. The camera phone always shows that the camera is on (Android: persistent notification; iOS: on-screen indicator). Detection runs on the phone, with no face recognition or identification. Detection box coordinates go to the viewer, and photos too if the detection photo feature is on.

2. What stays on your device

  • Recordings on the camera phone stay on that phone unless you upload them yourself.
  • The 20-second clip after a detection is stored on the camera phone only.
  • Wi-Fi connection details in the QR code are used only between the two phones.
  • Detection processing runs on the camera phone. Frames are not sent to our server for detection. Detection photos are sent only as described in section 1.

3. Location

PocketHomeCam does not collect location, and the camera phone's location is not sent to our server. Older Android versions require a location permission to create or find a hotspot; even then location is not collected or sent. Background location is not requested.

6. Retention and deletion

Retention periods are in section 1. When a period ends, we delete electronic files so they cannot be recovered. You can keep a recording by saving it to your phone before it expires. Share links open only with the six-digit code shown with them, lock after more than 8 wrong tries in 10 minutes, and expire after one hour; files a recipient has already downloaded cannot be recalled.

7. Your rights

  • You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop its processing, and you may withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Other rights under your local law also apply, and you may complain to your privacy regulator.
  • Use the app's account menu, the deletion page, or email help@pockethomecam.com from your account's address. We may ask for further confirmation.
  • We reply within the period your law sets, and at most 30 days. Exercising a right brings no disadvantage. A representative's request needs proof of authority. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already done.
  • Complaints: email us with what happened. We review it, reply with the outcome within 30 days, and you may then go to your privacy regulator.

8. Your responsibilities when filming

  • The Service is meant for your own home. Where other people pass through, follow local law and post a notice. The app can make a notice sign.
  • Do not film covertly, hide the indicator, or film other people's homes, bathrooms, changing rooms or other private spaces.
  • Telling carers, visitors and others in view that they are being filmed is your responsibility.
Not a medical or safety device. The Service does not measure breathing, heart rate or sleep and does not guarantee to prevent or detect accidents. Detection can miss or misidentify things, and video can drop with the network, device or server. Do not rely on it alone for the safety of a baby, patient or pet.

9. Security

  • Encryption in transit (TLS, DTLS-SRTP). Video passes through our relay, so it is not end-to-end encrypted.
  • Online Box recordings are encrypted at rest with AES-256, with keys stored apart from the recordings, and downloads and deletions are logged.
  • Access controls on our servers, and minimal administrative access. Sign-in sessions are excluded from device backups. Remote-viewing tokens allow camera controls only.
  • If a breach affects your data, we notify the competent authority and you without undue delay as the law requires.

10. Children

Accounts are for adults to create and manage, and we offer no accounts for children. A camera pointed at a child can capture that child in video or photos; filming and storage are then the parent or guardian's responsibility, and we delete the material when they delete it or ask us to. If we learn an account belongs to a child under 14, or under a higher age set by your country's law, we delete it and its data without delay.
