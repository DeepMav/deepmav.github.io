EEA·영국 이용자에게는 앱 최초 실행 시 광고 동의(UMP) 양식을 표시하며, 동의하지 않으면 맞춤 광고가 게재되지 않습니다. 이용자는 언제든 다음 권리를 행사할 수 있습니다.

  • 열람·정정·삭제·처리정지·이동 요청 — 본 앱은 서버에 개인정보를 보관하지 않으므로, 기기 설정에서 위치 권한을 해제하거나 앱을 삭제하면 즉시 중단·삭제됩니다.
  • 동의 철회 — 기기 설정에서 위치 권한을 해제하고, 광고 동의는 앱 내 광고 동의 양식에서 다시 설정할 수 있습니다.
  • 광고 식별자 재설정·삭제 — 안드로이드 [설정 > 개인정보 > 광고], iOS [설정 > 개인정보 보호 및 보안 > 추적].
  • 감독기구에 민원 제기 — 대한민국은 개인정보보호위원회(privacy.go.kr, 국번없이 182), EEA·영국은 거주 국가의 개인정보 감독기구.

English

FlyReady (“the App”) calculates local weather and flight suitability for drone pilots. The App has no sign-up and stores no personal data on any server of ours.

1. What we process, why, on what legal basis, and for how long

DataPurposeLegal basis (GDPR Art. 6)Retention
Location (latitude/longitude) Look up weather and geomagnetic conditions at your position and compute flight suitability Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — obtained through the device location permission Used for the request only; never stored
Advertising ID, device and ad-interaction data Ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention EEA/UK: consent via the UMP form. Elsewhere: as permitted by local law Per Google's retention policy
Aircraft profiles, app and notification settings Providing the App's features Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) On your device only; removed when you uninstall

The App collects no account data at all — no name, email, phone number, contacts or photos.

2. Data that stays on your device

Aircraft profiles (weight, battery, propellers and so on), calculation results, app settings and notification settings are stored only in on-device storage and are never transmitted. Uninstalling the App deletes all of them.

3. Third parties and international transfers

The App sends data to the third parties below. The weather providers log requests (source IP, requested coordinates) for their own purposes under their own policies; they are independent controllers, not processors acting on our behalf.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own advertising.

4. EEA/UK users and your rights

Users in the EEA and the UK are shown a Google UMP consent form on first launch; without consent, no personalised ads are served. You may exercise these rights at any time:

  • Access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability — because we hold no personal data on a server, revoking the location permission or uninstalling the App stops and erases everything immediately.
  • Withdraw consent — revoke the location permission in device settings; ad consent can be changed again from the consent form in the App.
  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID — Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads. iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — your national data protection authority in the EEA/UK; in Korea, the Personal Information Protection Commission (privacy.go.kr).

Write to harvey7926@gmail.com; we respond within 30 days.

5. Children under 13

The App is not directed to children under 13 (under 14 in Korea) and does not knowingly collect their personal data. If we learn that we have, we delete it immediately.

6. Security

All external requests use HTTPS. We operate no server of our own, so there is no stored personal data to secure.
